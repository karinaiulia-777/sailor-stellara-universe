# PRD – Sofia's Sailor Stellara Universe

## Project Overview

Create a magical, child-friendly, static website as a birthday surprise for Sofia, who is turning 9 years old.

The website should feel like a magical companion book inspired by the Sailor Moon anime series (German version) and specifically tailored to Sofia's imagination.

The central idea is that Sofia is not merely a fan of Sailor Moon.

She has been chosen to become her own Sailor Guardian:

**Sailor Stellara.**

The experience should combine:

* storytelling
* creativity
* exploration
* Japanese language learning
* music discovery
* character encyclopedia
* personal imagination

The website should feel magical, warm, playful, safe, and age-appropriate.
